Privacy Policy

Application Name: ASM Suite Player | Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Summary Guarantee

ASM Suite Player is built around strict data privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track your listening habits, and we do not collect your music files. All processing, metadata extraction, and equalizer audio synthesis occur entirely on your local device.

1. Information We Access and Why

ASM Suite Player requires access to specific device and cloud resources solely to deliver the media playback features requested by the user:

  • Google Drive API Data (Cloud Storage): When you choose to link your Google account, the application accesses your authorized Google Drive folders to search for audio/video files, read track metadata (such as ID3 titles, artist names, album titles, and embedded artwork), and stream audio data directly to your player.
  • Local Device Storage: Access to local media folders to play locally stored audio files, save custom equalizer presets, and cache offline tracks selected by you.

2. Google Drive API & OAuth 2.0 Disclosure

ASM Suite Player complies strictly with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

  • Requested Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly
  • Purpose of Access: Read-only access is used strictly to allow you to browse, search, stream, and download your audio files to your device for offline playback within ASM Suite Player.
  • No Data Transfer to Third Parties: The application does not upload, share, or transmit your Google Drive files, access tokens, or account profile information to any external server or third party.
  • No Modification: Because the requested scope is read-only, ASM Suite Player cannot modify, delete, or overwrite any files in your Google Drive account.

3. Data Storage and Retention

All application data (such as index databases, offline track caches, and user preferences) is stored locally on your device:

  • Local Indexing: Metadata extracted from your music files is stored in an encrypted local database on your device to enable fast offline searching.
  • OAuth Tokens: Authentication tokens are stored securely in your device's encrypted storage (KeyStore on Android / DPAPI on Windows).
  • Data Retention Period: Local data is retained until you sign out, reset the application library, clear app data, or uninstall the application.

4. Data Removal & How to Revoke Access

You retain full control over your data and permissions at all times:

  • In-App Removal: You can disconnect your Google Drive account anytime by opening Settings > Cloud > Sign Out or executing "Hard Reset Library" inside ASM Suite Player. This instantly purges all cached tokens and indexed metadata.
  • Google Account Revocation: You can revoke ASM Suite Player's access at any time by visiting your Google Account Third-Party Access Settings.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

ASM Suite Player does not use cookies, advertising networks, telemetry tools, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect telemetry, crash reports containing personal information, or listening analytics.

6. User Rights (GDPR & LGPD Compliance)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), users possess the right to:

  • Access, review, or delete any local data stored by the app on their device.
  • Revoke consent for Google Drive API access at any moment.
  • Request clarification regarding data processing practices.
