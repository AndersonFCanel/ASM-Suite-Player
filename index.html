ASM Suite Player

High-Fidelity Audio Player & Cloud Media Library for Google Drive

1. Music Library Selection

Allows users to browse and select personal audio folders in Google Drive to scan and index.

2. Metadata & Album Art

Reads ID3 tags, artist names, track numbers, and embedded cover art for library display.

3. Direct Audio Streaming

Streams lossless FLAC, WAV, MP3, and AAC audio directly into the app's studio audio engine.

4. Offline Caching

Enables pinning selected tracks for playback when device is offline without internet.

Data Protection & Zero Third-Party Sharing

ASM Suite Player operates entirely locally on your device. No audio files, tokens, or personal metadata are ever uploaded, sold, or shared with external servers or third parties.

Complete Studio Features

Designed for audiophiles and music lovers who demand pristine audio quality and cloud freedom.

Hi-Fi Audio

Lossless 32-bit float audio engine supporting FLAC, WAV, DSD, ALAC, MP3, and AAC with bit-perfect output capability.

Google Drive Integration

Seamlessly stream your cloud music collection directly from Google Drive with read-only OAuth authentication.

Offline Library

Playlists

How It Works

Simple, secure, and private access to your personal cloud music collection.

1

User Login

Launch ASM Suite Player on your device and initiate Google account authentication.

2

Authorize Google Drive Access

Grant read-only permission (drive.readonly) to allow the app to view and play your authorized audio files.

3

Select Music Folders

Choose which Google Drive folders contain your music library to index.

4

Library Synchronization

The app indexes file metadata locally on your device for fast searching and cover art rendering.

5

Cloud or Offline Playback

Choose whether to stream tracks directly from the cloud or pin them for offline enjoyment.

6

Direct High-Fidelity Playback

Enjoy studio-grade sound processed directly by the local audio engine on your hardware.
