## Google OAuth 2.0 Compliance

**ASM Suite Player** uses read-only Google Drive API scope (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly`) strictly to allow users to index, stream, and cache their personal audio collection from Google Drive onto their local device.

- Users retain 100% ownership of their music files.
- Zero audio files, metadata, or tokens are transferred to external third-party servers.
- Zero telemetry or analytics cookies are used.
